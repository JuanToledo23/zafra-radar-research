# Exercise 4b: multi-signal trained harvest detector vs exercise-4 detectors

Generated by `scripts/10_harvest_classifier.py`. Errors are in days, predicted date minus the midpoint of the Sentinel-2 NDVI harvest window (negative = early). Model dates are out-of-fold (GroupKFold by field, 5 folds). The rule runs on ascending HV, as in exercise 4. The ratio test runs on each orbit; its per-scene decisions, ascending and descending merged in time, go through the same two-in-a-row date rule as the models (~6-day sampling for both). The ascending-only ratio test of exercise 4 is a reference row in the summary.

**Scope.** The detector dates the harvest on fields already known to be sugarcane. Deciding whether a field is cane is a separate upstream step (next exercise). The false-alarm column below is the evidence for why that step is needed.

Training fields: 26 (`label == cana` with a harvest window). Negative control: 12 fields with another label (p26, p27, p28, p29, p30, p31, p32, p33a, p33b, p34, p35, p5). GPM rain feature: available.

* Missed fields are left out of the median and mean error and count as outside both tolerances.
* False alarms on non-cane fields: fields with any sustained detection in Nov-May.
* False alarms on cane fields outside Nov-May: **not measurable**. Every NISAR acquisition on disk is between 2025-11-18 and 2026-05-17, all inside the zafra.
