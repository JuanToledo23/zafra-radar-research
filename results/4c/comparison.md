# Exercise 4c: cane / non-cane gate

Generated by `scripts/11_cane_gate.py`. Method: `docs/METHODS.md`, section "Field selection (cane gate)".

**Circularity.** The cane labels come from Sentinel-2 NDVI phenology (script 07) plus visual tracing. Variant B and the NDVI rule are therefore partly re-learning the labelling rule, and their scores are optimistic by construction. Variant A (radar only) is the honest test; A+F adds VIIRS fire, also independent of NDVI.

**Operational gate: B (optical).** It drops no cane field; A drops 3, including p36, the only grower-confirmed field. Optical selects the fields, radar dates the harvest. `classify_fields` always also returns `p_cane_radar` (variant A) as the independent radar evidence. C is not recommended (no gain over B, drops p8).

Evaluation: leave-one-field-out on 38 fields, zafra 2025-26 (26 `cana` = positive; 12 others = negative: 11 `dudoso`, 1 `no_cana`). Threshold P >= 0.5. Rules have no training, so they are scored directly.
